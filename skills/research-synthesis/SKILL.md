# Research Synthesis

Build the answer from evidence rather than from a list of search results.

- Translate the question into distinct lines of inquiry before searching.
- Prefer primary sources; use secondary sources to add context or expose
  disagreement.
- Check dates whenever the answer may have changed.
- Keep claims attached to their supporting URLs throughout extraction and
  synthesis.
- Separate source statements from inference.
- Report meaningful contradictions, missing evidence, and confidence limits.
- End with the answer the evidence supports and the most useful next questions.

Do not pad the report with sources that do not affect the conclusion. Never
invent a citation or imply that a source was read when only its search snippet
was available.
